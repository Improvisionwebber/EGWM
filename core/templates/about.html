What We Believe

Our Core Beliefs

01

The Word

The Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God — our final authority for faith, doctrine and daily living.

02

Salvation

Salvation is by grace through faith in the finished work of Jesus Christ — freely offered to everyone who believes.

03

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit empowers believers for holy living, service, and the operation of spiritual gifts today.

04

Prayer

Prayer is the lifeline of the church. We gather to intercede, to seek God’s face, and to see His hand move.

05

Communion

We honour the Lord’s table as a place of remembrance, healing and covenant fellowship with Christ.

06

The Great Commission

Every believer is sent. We evangelise, disciple and serve our community with the love of Christ.
